Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to start?
Generally you need a valid government-issued ID, proof of address, and any previous DMV correspondence. Our advisors will provide a customized checklist after your initial verification.
How secure is my personal information?
We use bank-level encryption and never share data with third parties. All processes follow strict DMV guidelines and privacy laws.
Can I renew an expired license?
Yes, depending on the state and time since expiration. We assess your situation and determine eligibility. Most expired licenses under 2 years are renewable with a small late fee.

How to Use Our Online License Services

  1. Select Your Service: Choose the package that fits your needs.
  2. Submit Your Documents: Upload a copy of your ID, proof of residency, and any previous license via our secure portal.
  3. Verification: Our team will audit your documents within the selected timeframe and contact you with any missing items.
  4. DMV Filing: We submit the application directly to the DMV on your behalf, ensuring 100% compliance.
  5. Receive Your License: Once approved, we'll mail the physical copy to your address or you can pick it up at a local DMV office with our support.
